    <div class="space-y-4">
      <h2 class="text-2xl font-display font-extrabold text-white flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-emerald-500 text-xl font-mono">01/</span> Hybrid Retrieval
      </h2>
      <p>
        Dense embeddings (Sentence-Transformers <code>all-MiniLM-L6-v2</code>) find passages that mean the same thing. Okapi BM25 finds exact terms, such as a function name or an equation label, that embeddings blur. The two rankings are merged with Reciprocal Rank Fusion. Ingested documents keep their section hierarchy, so answers can point back to where they came from.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="space-y-4">

        <li class="flex items-start gap-3"><span class="text-emerald-500 mt-1">✓</span><div><strong class="text-white">Active recall:</strong> generated flashcards tagged by Bloom's taxonomy level and scheduled with the SM-2 algorithm.</div></li>
        <li class="flex items-start gap-3"><span class="text-emerald-500 mt-1">✓</span><div><strong class="text-white">Comparative matrix:</strong> synthesizes findings across several papers at once.</div></li>
