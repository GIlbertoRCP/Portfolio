    {/* Section 1: Overview & Privacy */}
    <div class="space-y-4">
      <h2 class="text-2xl font-display font-extrabold text-white flex items-center gap-2">
        <span class="text-blue-500 text-xl font-mono">01/</span> Privacy-First Local AI Architecture
      </h2>
      <p>
        Modern note-taking software increasingly relies on cloud-hosted LLM endpoints and server-side speech-to-text APIs. However, sending confidential meeting transcripts, academic research notes, or personal ideas to third-party cloud servers introduces significant data privacy risks and network latency bottlenecks.
      </p>
      <p>
        **WhispNotes** is a native macOS application designed for markdown note-taking with integrated on-device audio transcription and local Gemma 3 AI assistant capabilities. Designed for privacy and offline usage, **100% of note data, speech processing, and AI generation remain local to your machine**.
      </p>
    </div>
