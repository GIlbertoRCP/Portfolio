        <span class="text-blue-500 text-xl font-mono">01/</span> The Privacy &amp; Latency Challenge
      </h2>
      <p>
        Modern note-taking software increasingly relies on cloud-hosted LLM endpoints and server-side speech-to-text APIs. However, sending confidential meeting transcripts, academic research notes, or personal ideas to third-party cloud servers introduces significant data privacy risks, requires constant internet access, and suffers from network latency bottlenecks.
      </p>
      <p>
        **WhispNotes** eliminates cloud dependencies by executing **100% of speech transcription and LLM inference locally** on Apple Silicon hardware using Metal Performance Shaders and CoreML acceleration.
      </p>
